About me

My Story

I started racing go-karts at 14. From 14 to 17 I moved up through KA100 Junior, KA100 Senior, X30 Senior and KZ shifter karts, winning races in every class and championships in X30 and KZ.

To make the jump to cars, I spent the off-season working to fund my own Spec Miata, then built it at home in just under two months. In its first season that home-built car won 4 of the 8 races I entered, set the Spec Miata Toyo (SMT) track record at Road Atlanta, and took the HSR SSC Group 3 championship.

Then came a setback. My MX-5 Cup car was damaged in a fire, and I spent the next off-season and most of the following year rebuilding it from the ground up.

In 2026 I've focused on building partnerships and funding, with the goal of returning to the track full time in 2027.
